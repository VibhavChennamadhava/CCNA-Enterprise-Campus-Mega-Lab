# Spanning Tree Verification

Purpose:
Validate Rapid PVST+ and confirm STP root placement aligns with HSRP active gateway placement.

Commands to run:
show spanning-tree root
show spanning-tree vlan 10
show spanning-tree vlan 20
show spanning-tree vlan 30
show spanning-tree vlan 40
show spanning-tree vlan 99
show spanning-tree summary

Expected STP root placement:
Office A:
- DSW-A1 root for VLAN 10 and VLAN 99
- DSW-A2 root for VLAN 20 and VLAN 40

Office B:
- DSW-B1 root for VLAN 10 and VLAN 99
- DSW-B2 root for VLAN 20 and VLAN 30

Expected design reason:
The STP root bridge should align with the active HSRP gateway for that VLAN to keep traffic paths optimal.
